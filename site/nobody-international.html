Tell us your story

The proof

A school videographer sent one angry letter. His film won the Oscar.

Pavel Talankin ran events and filmed assemblies at Primary School No. 1 in Karabash, a small industrial town in Russia. Students came to his office to feel safe. His mother is the school librarian.

When the war in Ukraine began, his job changed. He was told to film children reciting support for the war and send the footage to the state.

He planned to resign. He wrote a furious letter instead. It reached director David Borenstein. Pavel stayed and kept filming for two years.

Then he left Russia with the footage. He is the film's co-director and he holds the Oscar for it. He now lives in exile in Europe.
